# Modern world screen UI

Status: approved direction (2026-10-18). This spec supersedes the world-screen parts of
`2026-10-12-civ6-atlas-ui-design.md` (the ordered nation ribbon and the permanent unit dock).

## Why

On the Atlas world screen, the map is buried under chrome:

- a 158-unit dark dock that paints even when nothing is selected
- a second header row that repeats the nation order
- the agent run state printed three times (turn button, control box, feed header)
- thick parchment border ribbons that overshoot every hex corner
- 300-wide city plaques

The goal is a calm, modern app look. The map is the content. Panels float, hug their
content, and appear only when they have something to show.

## Principles

1. **Panels earn their place.** A panel with nothing in it is hidden, not drawn empty.
   No region-sized backgrounds. A card is sized to its content.
2. **Say it once.** Each fact (run state, current nation, model) has one home.
3. **Float, don't slab.** Rounded translucent cards with an 8-unit margin from the screen
   edge. The map stays visible between them, and the gaps pass input through to the map.
4. **No nested cards.** A card never sits inside another card.
5. **Quiet controls.** Secondary controls have no bright outline. Brass marks only the one
   primary action and selected state.
6. **Legacy untouched.** Every change is gated on `GameUiStyle.isAtlas`. Minimal and mod
   skins render exactly as before. Other screens (city, tech, diplomacy, overview pages,
   menus) are out of scope, and skin-wide button defaults stay as they are.

## Visual language (Atlas, world screen only)

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Panel radius | 12 units |
| Control radius | 8 units |
| Chip / pill | fully rounded |
| Panel fill | `surface` #10232F at 0.90 opacity (0.94 for text-dense sheets) |
| Hairline rim | 1 unit, `ink` at about 9% alpha |
| Shadow | soft halo, blur about 10 units, offset about 3 units downward, black at about 35% |
| Edge margin / gap | 8 units (`Space`) |
| Type | existing `GameUiStyle.Type` scale. Primary text `ink`, secondary `muted`. No new fonts. |
| Status dot | `info` running, `warning` paused or pausing, `error` failed or needs attention, `muted` idle or finished |

Art is generated by a committed script (`scripts/atlas/gen_ui_ninepatches.py`). It produces
2x 9-patches in `android/Images.Skin/Skins/Atlas/`: `surface`, `surface-rim`,
`surface-shadow`, `control-r8`, `control-r8-rim`, `pill`, `pill-rim`. They are packed into
the committed `Skin.atlas`/`Skin.png`.

`GameUiStyle` gains `surface(path, opacity, elevated)`, `pill(path, color)` and quiet/ghost
button roles. These are exposed through `WorldScreenStyle`. When not Atlas, every helper
falls back to today's drawables.

## Layout (wide, at least 960 units)

```
 [≡][◉ America ▾]   [gold +3 · sci +8 · ☺ 1 · ♪ +1]   [T32 · 2720 BC][Overview]
                                                             [notifications]
 [tech · policy chips]
 ┌ activity ┐
 │  sheet   │                  map                     ┌ minimap ┐
 │          │                                          └─────────┘
 └──────────┘          [ selection card ]              [ agent card / turn ]
```

- **Header: 56 × fontScale** (8 margin + 40 cluster + 8). Compact adds the 44-unit tab strip.
  The top bar becomes three floating clusters on `surface()`. The bar itself is unpainted.
  - **Left:** menu icon button, then the identity chip (24 portrait, name at 15pt, ellipsis,
    max about 200). Spectators get a dropdown: "Follow active nation", then the nation
    roster in turn order. Picking a nation calls `inspectNation`; "Follow" clears the
    inspection. The chip never grants control. The caption row is removed; the era moves
    to a tooltip.
  - **Centre:** stats inline (16 icon + value at 14pt). Rates are muted and hidden when
    zero. No "(0/335)", no "(∞)". Resources with an amount of zero are hidden. A spectator
    sees the followed or inspected nation's economy, with a "Following" or "Viewing" caption
    on the identity chip. If there is no such nation, the cluster is hidden. It never shows
    the Spectator civ's zeros.
  - **Right:** turn chip "T32 · 2,720 BC" (muted, same click target as today), the supply
    warning only on deficit, then Overview.
  - **Narrow widths** drop items by priority: resources, then stat suffixes, then the centre
    cluster.
- **The nation ribbon is removed.** `NationTurnRoster` moves to its own file. With exactly
  two nations, the other nation is "Next in order", not "Previous in order".
- **Tracker** (tech, policy, diplomacy, espionage, undo, fog, chat) sits top-left under the
  header. It is content-sized and hidden when empty.
- **Activity sheet:** left side, from under the tracker (or header) to the bottom margin.
  Open by default in agent matches. Slim header, no footer (see Agent match).
- **Selection card:** bottom centre, centred in the band between the sheet (only when open)
  and the right column. Content-sized. Hidden unless a unit, city or spy is selected; the
  idle-units summary alone does not open it. No outer dock background: the UnitTable
  surface is the card.
- **Right column** (about 280–300 wide). From the bottom: the turn action, sized to its
  content (agent card or turn button); the minimap card above it; notifications from below
  the header down to above the minimap.
- **Battle preview** sits directly above the selection card.
- **Compact (under 960):** keeps the tab model and tab names. On the World tab, the
  selection card floats above the turn action whenever something is selected.

`WorldHudLayout` stays pure. It gains a content-aware overload: tracker size, selection
size, activity open, turn-action size. The existing 4-argument overload stays as a
conservative default.

## Agent match

- **One agent card in the turn slot.** It replaces the disabled debug-string turn button,
  the "Control" toggle and the floating control box.
  - Row 1: status dot, "Paused · America" (14pt ink), elapsed time (12pt muted,
    right-aligned, label name `AgentRunElapsed`).
  - Row 2: model caption "GPT-6.1 Sol · Medium" (11pt muted, ellipsis).
  - Row 3: primary Pause/Resume/Retry when available, then compact quiet buttons
    "Activity", "Messages", "Settings" (accent-tinted while their sheet is open). When a
    human may act, `NextTurnButton` takes the primary cell.
- **One status vocabulary.** `AgentStatusFormat` is the single source of state words and
  colours, shared by the card, the feed and the overview. The turn-button text becomes
  "state · nation". The full `statusText()` stays in logs and tooltips only.
- **Feed.** The header is one row: nation chip ▾, Logs, close. The identity, elapsed and
  state rows are removed; a single muted line appears only when the filter shows a nation
  other than the active one. There is no footer:
  - "Earlier" becomes a quiet row at the top of the list, shown only when older history
    exists.
  - "Live" becomes a floating "↓ N new" chip, shown only when not following.
  - In follow mode, the speaker name appears once per turn heading; blocks get kind-only
    captions (Tools, Thinking, Run completed).
- **Messages and Settings** open as right-side sheets below the header, above the minimap.
  Both close with the same X.

## Map

- **Borders** (AtlasTerrain tileset). Regenerate the 8 border sprites at 128×24, with the
  hex edge spanning x 32–96 on the bottom row:
  - Outer: a thin solid line about 3 px (about 2 world units), tinted by code with the
    nation's inner colour.
  - Inner: a soft tint about 15 px deep, fading from about 38% to 0 alpha, tinted with the
    nation's outer colour.
  - Mitred ends: a concave end follows the hex side; a convex end follows the corner
    bisector.
  - No Kotlin change. Repack `AtlasTerrain.atlas`, stop `normalize_sources.py` from
    regenerating ribbons, and refresh the provenance hashes.
- **City banner** (Atlas only). One row, a pill about 24 tall:
  - Contents: pop · ★ · name (15pt, one line, ellipsis at about 150) · religion ·
    construction icon 16 + turns 11 · nation disc 16.
  - Growth and production become 2-unit bars along the bottom inner edge.
  - Defence shows as a chip only when the city is damaged or at war. Air units show as a
    small corner chip. Health is a 2-unit top bar, shown only when damaged.
  - The legacy banner and the popup variant are unchanged.
- **Minimap card:** rounded surface. The five layer toggles move into a Layers popover in
  the card corner. No drag handle on Atlas.
- **Tile info:** hidden when empty. When a tile is selected, it shows as a compact card
  above the minimap.
- **Notifications (Atlas):** one drawer surface.
  - Header: "Notifications · N" plus a Hide control.
  - Muted category captions with no white rules. Plain rows with no per-item cards. 20-unit
    icons without plates.
  - Bell: a 36-unit quiet icon button with an accent count badge, hidden at zero, anchored
    in the right column.

## Invariants that must hold

- Spectator nation inspection only calls `WorldScreen.inspectNation`. Never a session,
  controller, takeover or vision API.
- A non-spectator never sees other civs' agent activity or names.
- The agent timer tick stays isolated (no extra session calls, no redraw when unchanged).
- Hidden panes keep their actors alive. Keyboard shortcuts and selection state survive.
  Hide with `isVisible = false`; never recreate gameplay actors.
- The turn action stays reachable and last in z-order. During a human turn, `NextTurnButton`
  is never hidden.
- At least 45% of the map is unobstructed at 1440×900 at 1× and 1.5× (target 60% or more).
  Body text contrast is at least 4.5:1.
